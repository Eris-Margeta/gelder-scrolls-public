Does it edit or upload my notes?

Gelder Scrolls is a read-only viewer. Document processing happens on your device. Images and links in a document may contact their destinations when loaded or opened. The app’s soundscape is available offline.

Is it really free?

Yes. The project is intended to be free to download and use, without an account or subscription. See the repository license for source and redistribution terms.

How will I install it?

When the verified Mac release is available, download the DMG, open it, and drag Gelder Scrolls into Applications. We will publish signing, notarization, and checksum details alongside the download. There is no need to disable Gatekeeper.

Who made the music?

“Ever Mindful” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The recording is encoded with gentle endpoint fades. Rain and night ambience are original synthesis.
