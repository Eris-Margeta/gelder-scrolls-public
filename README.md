# Gelder Scrolls

### A quieter place to read Markdown.

Ordinary notes. Old parchment. A candlelit desk.

**Free desktop reader · Local files · No account required**

</div>

Gelder Scrolls turns Markdown documents into a reading experience at a scholar’s desk: textured parchment, ornate wooden rollers, warm candlelight, and a quiet optional soundscape. Your original files stay untouched.

## Release status

Public release engineering is in progress. A verified, notarized macOS download will be linked here when ready. Windows and Linux desktop builds are also in development; they are not yet available downloads.

We do not recommend bypassing Gatekeeper or running unsigned development artifacts as a substitute for an official release.

## The experience

- Read local `.md`, `.markdown`, and `.mdown` documents.
- Switch between manuscript and regular text presentation.
- Let the paper rolls show your reading progress without a conventional scrollbar.
- Adjust your viewpoint and mix quiet music, rain, and night ambience.
- Keep short notes physically small: extra zoom does not create extra paper.

## Privacy

Document processing is local. Remote images and links referenced by your document may contact their destination when loaded or opened. Background audio is bundled for offline playback. No account or analytics service is required to read.
